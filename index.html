Before you
flash.

Everything you need to know before installing AstralOS.

What devices are supported?

Currently only Xiaomi Redmi Note 9 Pro (miatoll). This includes all miatoll variants: curtana, joyeuse, excalibur, and gram. More devices may be added in the future.

How do I install AstralOS?

Flash the latest LineageOS recovery for miatoll, then sideload the AstralOS zip via ADB. A full wipe is recommended when coming from stock MIUI or another ROM. Detailed instructions will be provided with each release.

Do I need to unlock the bootloader?

Yes. Unlocking the bootloader is required before flashing any custom ROM. Use Xiaomi's official unlock tool or your preferred method.

Will OTA updates work?

AstralOS includes a built-in updater. Updates will be pushed through the OTA system as new builds are released.

Where do I get help?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository or reach out through the community channels.

Is Google services included?

No. AstralOS does not ship with Google Apps. You can flash a GApps package of your choice after installing the ROM, or use it de-Googled.
